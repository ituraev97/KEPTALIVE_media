What is the "golden hour" in trauma care, and how do military medevac crews race it? We follow Army Dustoff, National Guard and Air Force crews in training, from the 9-line radio call to the hoist, and look at where the idea came from: R Adams Cowley's "golden hour", helicopter evacuation in Korea and Vietnam, the Pentagon's 2009 60-minute rule for Afghanistan, and what the research does and doesn't show.
