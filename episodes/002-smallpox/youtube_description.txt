How do you erase a disease from an entire planet? Smallpox killed about 3 in 10 of the people who caught it, for thousands of years. We follow how it was beaten: variolation in Boston in 1721, George Washington's 1777 order to inoculate the Continental Army, Edward Jenner's cowpox vaccine, the WHO's 1967 Intensified Eradication Program, the bifurcated needle and ring vaccination, the last natural case in 1977, and the two official virus stocks that remain today.
