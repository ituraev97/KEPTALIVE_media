How do you build a hospital that can sail anywhere, and switch it on in five days? USNS Comfort is a converted supertanker with a 1,000-bed hospital, 12 operating rooms and its own oxygen plants. We follow it from the Gulf War and 9/11 to the Haiti earthquake, Hurricane Maria, its month in New York in 2020, and the 79-day Continuing Promise 2025 mission that treated 12,616 patients in six countries.
