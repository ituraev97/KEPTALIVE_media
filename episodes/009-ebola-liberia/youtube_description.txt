How was the 2014 Ebola outbreak stopped in Liberia? The West Africa Ebola epidemic was the largest Ebola outbreak in history, and in September 2014 Monrovia had almost no treatment beds. This medical history documentary follows Operation United Assistance, the U.S. military's mobile labs, Ebola treatment units and the Monrovia Medical Unit, and what the evidence says really turned the tide.

The U.S. military did not treat Ebola patients. Cases in Liberia were already falling before most U.S.-built units opened, and studies credit Liberian communities, safe burial teams, contact tracing and isolation. Then: the Ebola vaccine and treatments approved since 2019, and the 2026 Bundibugyo outbreak in Congo, with CDC figures. History and public-health facts only; not medical advice.
