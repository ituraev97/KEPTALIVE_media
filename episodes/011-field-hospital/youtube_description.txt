How does the Army build a field hospital in 72 hours? In a 2017 Army test, hospital units deployed from Colorado to Fort Polk, Louisiana, and had hospital care with surgery and intensive care running within 72 hours. This military medicine documentary explains how a field hospital works, from the Korean War MASH (mobile army surgical hospital) to today's 32-bed Army field hospital.

With real 1951 U.S. Army film of the 8209th MASH in Korea, the Army's last MASH (2006), field hospitals built after Hurricane Maria in Puerto Rico and for COVID-19 in Seattle and New York, and what Army medics are training for in 2026: beds as the bottleneck, drones, and hospitals underground. History and public facts only.
