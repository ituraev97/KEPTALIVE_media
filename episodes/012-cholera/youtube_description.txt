How did one doctor trace a deadly cholera outbreak to a single water pump? In 1854, more than 500 people died of cholera in ten days around the Broad Street pump in London. This cholera outbreak documentary tells how John Snow followed the water house by house, and why the real story is better than the famous myth.

With John Snow's own 1855 maps and tables, the skeptic clergyman who found the leaking cesspool, the "grand experiment" of two London water companies, how chlorinated drinking water changed America, a 1943 U.S. Army training film on purifying water, and cholera today according to the CDC and WHO. History and public facts only.
