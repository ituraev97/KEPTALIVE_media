## G. Notes outside today's scope (not blocking)

- episode.py lines 357–358 say "Union surgeons recorded 29,980 amputations … Counting both armies … 50,000–60,000". "Union surgeons recorded" is accurate. But because 29,980 already includes Confederate patients, adding a separate Confederate estimate may double-count some cases. Consider "perhaps 50,000 or more" or keep it, since it is labelled an estimate.
- episode.py line 68: "GUNSHOT WOUNDS TREATED · UNION ARMY" would be more accurate as "· UNION ARMY RECORDS" (see #1).
- sources.md #40 calls 97.4% Union mortality from pyemia. Tag it as "in Union records".
