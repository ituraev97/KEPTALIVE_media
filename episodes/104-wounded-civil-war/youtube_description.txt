What being wounded in the Civil War actually felt like for a Union soldier — from the moment a soft lead Minié ball hit, to the stretcher, the field hospital, chloroform and the surgeon's saw, infection before germ theory, Gettysburg's Camp Letterman, Washington's hospitals and the long road home. Civil War medicine told from the Army's own Medical and Surgical History of the War of the Rebellion and the words of the people who were there.

WAR MEDICINE · EP 2. Wartime photographs and engravings; no wounds on screen; history only.
