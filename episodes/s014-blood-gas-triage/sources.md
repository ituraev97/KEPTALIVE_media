## s2 — WWI gas cases
- U.S. Army Heritage and Education Center, "Gas Warfare in World War I": https://www.armyheritage.org/soldier-stories-information/gas-warfare-in-world-war-i/
  - "In the A.E.F., 70,522 cases related to gas exposure were reported, 31.49 per cent of all casualties. Of this number, 1,221 died, the majority from mustard gas exposure." (2,853 more invalided home.)
- Army Nurse Corps Association, "Mustard gas, WWI": https://e-anca.org/History/Topics-in-ANC-History/Mustard-WWI
  - "more than 30% of the casualties were related to chemical warfare"; deaths "For American forces it was 2% of all gas related injuries".
  - Decontamination: removing uniforms, bathing, issuing new clothing before hospital treatment.
- Background only: NLM Circulating Now "Gassed" (2017): chemical weapons ~1.3 million casualties incl. 91,000 deaths across all combatants, ~5% of the war's casualties; clothing removed and washed. https://circulatingnow.nlm.nih.gov/2017/10/04/gassed

## s3 — DARPA Triage Challenge finals
- DARPA, Triage Challenge — Finals page: https://www.darpa.mil/research/challenges/darpa-triage-challenge/finals

  - Systems teams use autonomous/partly autonomous drones and ground robots to find casualties, identify life-threatening injuries, perform trauma assessments, capture vital signs from a distance, share information with human medics. Technologies to "save lives on and off the battlefield."
