## s1 — Desmond Doss
- Medal of Honor citation, PFC Desmond T. Doss, 307th Infantry, 77th Infantry Division, near Urasoe-Mura, Okinawa, 29 April – 21 May 1945: "assaulted a jagged escarpment 400 feet high … inflicting approximately 75 casualties and driving the others back. PFC Doss refused to seek cover … carrying them one by one to the edge of the escarpment and there lowering them on a rope-supported litter down the face of a cliff to friendly hands." — The AMEDD Historian, no. 1 (Winter 2013), pp. 6–7 (U.S. Army AMEDD Regiment; archive.org TheAMEDDHistorianIssue1.1, Public Domain Mark) https://archive.org/details/TheAMEDDHistorianIssue1.1 (text: https://archive.org/download/TheAMEDDHistorianIssue1.1/The%20AMEDD%20Historian%20issue%201.1_djvu.txt); same text on the National WWII Museum page below. Original: NARA RG 338, NAID 595188 (docsteach.org/document/doss-citation/)
- "PFC Desmond T. Doss is the only known conscientious objector to receive the Medal of Honor in World War II." — The AMEDD Historian no. 1, p. 6. "his faith restricted him from taking a life" — same.
- "By nightfall, he had rescued 75 soldiers"; "Dear God, let me get just one more man."; "Seventh Day Adventist"; "chose not to bear arms"; "400-foot-high jagged cliff, nicknamed Hacksaw Ridge"; "President Harry Truman placed the Medal of Honor around his neck" — K. Lange, DoD News, army.mil, Feb. 28, 2017 https://www.army.mil/article/183328
- "Doss successfully rescued 75 men trapped at the top of the escarpment"; "On October 12, 1945, President Harry S. Truman presented Doss with the Medal of Honor"; "the first--and only--conscientious objector to receive the Medal of Honor during World War II"; "He had removed any markings indicating he was medic" — K. Martin, National WWII Museum, Oct. 12, 2020 https://www.nationalww2museum.org/war/articles/private-first-class-desmond-thomas-doss-medal-of-honor
- "refused to kill enemy soldiers or carry a weapon because of his religious beliefs as a Seventh-day Adventist" — National Archives DocsTeach https://docsteach.org/document/doss-citation/
- Photo: Cpl. Desmond T. Doss wearing the Medal of Honor (corporal's chevrons; 1945) — The AMEDD Historian no. 1, p. 6 (U.S. Army publication, Public Domain Mark on archive.org)
- Footage: DVIDS 766514 "Chaplains discuss Desmond Doss' resilience during the Battle of Hacksaw Ridge" — Sgt. Raquel Birk, 38th Air Defense Artillery Brigade, Hacksaw Ridge, Okinawa, Japan (published 2020-09-21).
