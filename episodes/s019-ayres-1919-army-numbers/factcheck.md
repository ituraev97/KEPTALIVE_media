Fixes made during the check:

2. s2: the "two of every 100 killed or died of disease" line is item 1 of the summary (all soldiers and sailors, whole war) — wording kept as the source's.
3. Dropped the Spanish-American War "85 per cent" typhoid line (ambiguous denominator).
4. s1: 5 per 1,000 is per YEAR (to Sept 14, 1918), 4 per 1,000 is in ONE WEEK; both stated as such; "at home" = troops in this country.
5. s3: 19 is the expeditionary-forces rate to Nov 11, 1918; "overseas in World War One" says so.
Residual caveats: all numbers come from one 1919 source (revised to Aug 1, 1919) — later official tallies differ (e.g. Army KIA/died-of-wounds figures in other sources); the Shorts attribute every number to "the Army's 1919 summary".
